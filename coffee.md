# Coffee recipes

One recipe is featured on the dashboard each day (they rotate).
Each recipe is a `##` heading, then `- Key: value` lines, then numbered steps.

## V60 (Hoffmann)
- Coffee: 15 g, medium-fine
- Water: 250 g at 93–100 °C
- Ratio: 1:16.7
- Time: 3:00–3:30

1. Bloom with 30 g water, swirl, wait 45 s
2. Pour to 150 g by 1:15
3. Pour to 250 g by 1:45
4. Stir gently, swirl, let drain

## AeroPress (inverted)
- Coffee: 17 g, medium
- Water: 220 g at 90 °C
- Ratio: 1:13
- Time: 2:00

1. Add coffee, pour all water, stir 10 s
2. Cap and steep until 1:30
3. Flip and press for 30 s

## French Press
- Coffee: 30 g, coarse
- Water: 500 g just off the boil
- Ratio: 1:16.7
- Time: 9:00

1. Pour all water, wait 4 min
2. Break the crust, skim the foam
3. Wait 5 more min, plunge to just the surface, pour
